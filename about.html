About Ramika Safaris and Tours

A Tanzanian safari company planning journeys from the southern highlands outward – with clarity, licensed vehicles, and lodging arranged to the standard you choose.

Who we are

Across Tanzania

A southern start

Ramika Safaris and Tours Limited was incorporated in 2020 under the Companies Act of 2002 (registration number 141390732). Our head office is in Njombe Town, with supporting desks in Iringa, Dodoma, Arusha, Mbeya, Dar es Salaam, Morogoro, and Zanzibar.

We are registered with the Ministry of Tourism. Our safari vehicles are licensed and insured for guest travel. We begin where we know the land best – southern Tanzania – and plan northern, western, or coastal routes whenever they belong in your journey.

Our approach

Why travel with us

Clear planning. Grounded travel.

  • Home in the south

    Southern highlands, Kitulo, Mikumi, and neighbouring parks are the routes we know first – and plan with care.

  • One company, wider Tanzania

    Northern and western itineraries are arranged by the same team whenever they belong in your journey – not as an afterthought.

  • Licensed vehicles & booked lodging

    Safari vehicles are licensed and insured. Lodging is reserved to the standard you choose – luxury, budget, or economy – at independently owned properties.

  • Registered & reachable

    We are registered with the Ministry of Tourism, with desks across major towns so planning stays practical from enquiry to departure.
